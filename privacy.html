Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 26, 2026 · ExpoLeap Workshops™ LLC · EIN 66-1133450

Short version: We only receive information you choose to send us directly by email. We do not use tracking cookies, analytics services, or advertising tools. We never sell your information.

1. Who We Are

ExpoLeap Workshops™ LLC ("ExpoLeap," "we," "our," or "us") is a professional development company based in Puerto Rico, offering academic research and educational technology workshops. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in connection with our website at expoleap.app and our workshop services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only what you send us directly:

  • Booking inquiries: When you click a "Book This Workshop" link, your email client opens a pre-addressed email. Any information you include in that email (name, institution, preferred dates, questions) is received by us at expo.leap.workshops@gmail.com.
  • Direct correspondence: Any emails you send us for support, questions, or information.

We do not operate a sign-up form, account system, or any server-side data collection on this website.

3. Cookies and Tracking

Our website does not use tracking cookies, advertising pixels, or analytics services. We do not collect IP addresses, device identifiers, or browsing behavior.

Web fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. Google may collect limited technical data as part of that service, subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

4. How We Use Your Information

Information you send us by email is used solely to:

  • Respond to your inquiry or booking request
  • Coordinate workshop scheduling, logistics, and payment
  • Send follow-up materials you requested

We will not add you to any mailing list or marketing communication without your explicit consent.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties, except:

  • When required by law or valid legal process
  • With your explicit consent

Your email is stored by Gmail (Google LLC), subject to Google's terms and privacy practices.

6. Data Retention

We retain email correspondence for as long as it is reasonably needed for business purposes — typically for the duration of any active engagement and a reasonable period afterward for reference. You may request deletion of your correspondence at any time.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request a copy of any personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of that information
  • Withdraw any consent previously given

To exercise any of these rights, email us at expo.leap.workshops@gmail.com.

8. Children's Privacy

Our website is directed to educational institutions and adult professionals, not to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will post the new version on this page with a revised effective date. We encourage you to review this page if you have any ongoing concern about how your information is handled.
